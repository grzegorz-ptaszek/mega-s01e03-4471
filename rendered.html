The #4471 page says the 412 merged reports are one parser bug with one fix queued. They are not. The desk’s own rules (causes.ts) put them in two causes, and the desk’s own notes (desk-notes.json) set five reports aside that fit neither.

Not known. Fixing the parser closes one of three problems. The other two stay open and have no ticket of their own.

The three problems

  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get flipped dates. This is the problem #4471 describes.
  • hint. Reports through the booking-v3 form, from the three tenants that run it. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY, so people type month first. A parser fix does not change it.
  • one-day shift. Five reports of a date one day off, or an invite that arrived before the booking. A month-first parser cannot do this. The cause is not confirmed; a time-zone error is the first guess.

5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

The table counts the five one-day reports under parser, because that is where their tenant files them. That is why parser reads five higher here than above.

How to tell them apart

The cause depends on the tenant (its locale and its booking form), not on the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the guess is wrong often: some reports tagged hint belong to the parser, and some tagged parser come through the hint.

Lessons

  • Merge on the cause, not on the subject. A subject says what the reporter saw, not what broke.
  • Count a report once. The ticket export pages overlap, so a report can come twice; one id is one report.
  • A count that tells engineering how big the work is must be the count for one problem.
